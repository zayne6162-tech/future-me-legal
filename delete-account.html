Delete your account

Two ways to do it. The first is immediate; the second is for when you cannot use the app.

In the app — immediate

1

Open Future Me and go to Profile.

2

Tap Delete account.

3

Enter your password and type DELETE to confirm.

Everything is removed straight away. There is no waiting period and no recovery, which is the point.

By email

If you have lost the app or the device, email us from the address your account uses — that is how we know the request is yours — with the subject Delete my account.

support@futuremelabs.example

We will confirm within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30, usually much sooner. We will not ask you for anything else first.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your profile, goal and every training plan you have had
  • Every workout, set, rep and weight you logged
  • Every daily check-in, including your notes
  • Your weight history and body measurements
  • Every progress photo, removed from storage
  • Your entire coach conversation
  • Your subscription record, deleted from RevenueCat

All of it, permanently. We keep no copy and there is no archive to restore from.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription.

Google Play bills subscriptions, not us, and only you can cancel one. Cancel it first in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions — otherwise you will keep being charged for an account that no longer exists.

What we keep, and why

Records of payments, where tax or accounting law requires us to hold them. These contain no training data, no body data and no photographs — only that a transaction happened.
