Privacy Policy

Future Me · Last updated 16 September 2026

Future Me is a training app. It holds what you tell it about your training and your body so it can build you a plan and show you what has changed. This page says exactly what that means.

What we collect

What Why
Email address To sign you in. It is the only contact detail we hold.
Age range, height, weight, training experience, equipment, dietary preference To build a plan that fits you.
Your goal, in your own words To build a plan aimed at it.
Workouts you log — sessions, exercises, sets, reps, weights, optional notes To track consistency and show your strength over time.
Daily check-ins — energy, sleep, soreness, motivation, hours slept, and an optional note To show trends and to adapt your plan.
Body measurements — weight and optional waist, chest, arm, thigh To chart change over time.
Progress photos, if you add them To show you side by side. Nothing else.
Messages you send the coach To answer them, and to keep the conversation.
Subscription status and purchase history To know what you have paid for.

What we do not do

Your progress photos are never analysed.

They are stored privately, shown back to you behind links that expire after five minutes, and read by nothing — not by us, not by any AI provider, not on your phone. There is no physique scoring, no body-fat estimate, and no body-composition inference anywhere in this app, from photographs or from any other data.

We also do not:

  • Sell your data, or share it with advertisers.
  • Send your check-in notes or your workout notes to any AI provider.
  • Make medical claims, or attempt to diagnose anything.

Who else sees it

Who What they get
Anthropic — writes your plan, weekly review and coach replies Your profile and goal, a summary of your plan, your logged training, your check-in scores, and the coach messages you send. Not your check-in notes, not your workout notes, and never your photos.
RevenueCat — handles subscriptions Your account identifier and your purchase history. No training or body data.
Supabase — hosts the database and storage Everything, as the processor that stores it on our behalf. They do not use it for anything of their own.
Google Play — takes the payment Whatever a purchase requires. We never see your card.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. We do not expire your data, and we do not delete anything because a subscription ended — if you stop paying, your plans, logs, check-ins, measurements and photos all stay exactly where they are.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. It is immediate and cannot be undone. Your photos are removed from storage, your subscription record is deleted from RevenueCat, and every row belonging to you is deleted from the database.

If you cannot use the app, request deletion here.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, not by us, and only you can cancel one — in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Please cancel before deleting your account, or you will continue to be charged for something you can no longer use.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Most of it you can edit in the app directly. For anything else, write to us at the address below and we will answer.

Children

Future Me is not for under-18s, and sign-up asks your age range and stops there if you are under 18. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who receives it, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
